# Research & Documentation Hub

Use this space to capture long-form write-ups, presentation decks, and research notes. Suggested subfolders:

- `/incident-response/` – Post-incident reviews, tabletop exercise findings, playbooks
- `/malware-analysis/` – Reverse engineering notes, YARA rules, sandbox observations
- `/talks/` – Conference slide decks, speaker notes, demo materials

## Suggested Template for Documentation

```markdown
# Title

## Abstract
Brief summary of the research or engagement.

## Background
Context required to understand the problem space.

## Methodology
Step-by-step approach, including tools and configurations.

## Findings
Detailed results with screenshots, data tables, or diagrams.

## Recommendations
Actionable guidance or mitigations derived from the work.

## References
Relevant links, standards, or supporting documentation.
```

Keep the documentation professional and include visuals, diagrams, or timelines where they strengthen the narrative.
